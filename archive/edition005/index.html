Cosa ResourcesTSXV:COSA14 Sep

Announces Summer Drilling Results for Murphy Lake North Uranium Joint Venture with Denison Mines

The week's only new chemical assay, and its largest continuous intercept. Hole MLN26-013 returned 5.0 m averaging 0.55% U₃O₈ from 308.5-313.5 m, including a higher-grade 0.5 m at 1.70% U₃O₈. The hole sits on the Cyclone trend, roughly 3 km east of IsoEnergy's Hurricane deposit, on a project Cosa operates as a 70% joint venture with Denison Mines (30%). The 13-hole, 6,752 m program tested 650 m of strike along the trend and outlined two basement fault structures - the N-fault and S-fault - both only partly tested at the unconformity, with several hundred metres of strike left open in multiple directions. Six other holes returned handheld spectrometer readings rather than assays: MLN26-018A (315 cps), MLN26-024 (250 cps), MLN26-026 (two intervals, 340 cps each), MLN26-027 (320 cps), MLN26-029 (275 cps) and MLN26-030 (300 cps) - counts-per-second on core, not assays, and not a percentage. Cosa reports approximately $16 million in treasury and plans winter drilling at Darby and Aurora before returning to Murphy Lake North in early 2027. Denison Mines has not issued its own release on the joint venture.

Purepoint UraniumTSXV:PTU10 Sep

Purepoint Reports Winter 2026 Assays from Dorado JV's Nova Target

Still a chemical assay, not just a gamma reading. Nine holes at the Nova discovery on the 50/50 Dorado JV (with IsoEnergy), eastern basin margin. Best result is NV26-05, 0.3 m at 1.65% U₃O₈ from 390.4-390.7 m, paired with a 61,070 cps average / 73,070 cps peak on the downhole Mount Sopris 2PGA-1000 probe. NV26-03A returned a broader 3.9 m at 0.04% U₃O₈ (412.6-416.5 m) with a 0.4 m higher-grade inclusion at 0.10% U₃O₈, plus a separate 0.4 m interval at 0.79% U₃O₈ (37,050 cps) from 418.2-418.6 m. Seven more holes (NV26-01, -02, -04, -06A, -07, -08, -09A) returned lower-grade mineralization, 0.01-0.07% U₃O₈. Collars are tabulated in NAD83 UTM Zone 13 (e.g. NV26-05 at 548809E, 6475821N). An earlier release from this same winter program (14 April) reported the gamma-probe readings alone with assays pending; this release is the assay follow-through on those same holes. The release ties results to previous Nova drilling of up to 8.1% U₃O₈ over 0.4 m within a broader 1.62% U₃O₈ over 2.1 m, and describes a mineralized corridor extending roughly one kilometre. The summer program at Nova has also been completed, with those results still pending.

IsoEnergyTSX:ISO8 Sep

IsoEnergy Completes Summer Drilling at Larocque East, Intersecting Widespread and Strongest Radioactivity to Date Along the Hurricane South Trend

26 holes for 10,159 m, and every number in the release is a counts-per-second reading, not a grade. The program overran its 8,000 m / 20-hole plan and brings 2026 to 16,963 m in 43 holes. Best intervals are LE26-250 on the West L Fault, 3.5 m at 11,075 cps from 341.0 m including 0.5 m at 43,160 cps; LE26-273, 3.0 m at 14,135 cps from 328.0 m including 1.0 m at 36,292 cps; and LE26-254 on the East L Fault, 2.0 m at 10,110 cps from 331.5 m. Eighteen of the 26 holes returned at least 350 cps over half a metre, 13 exceeded 1,000 cps. These are handheld RS-125 spectrometer totals taken on core - not a chemical assay, and not calibrated downhole gamma either, so they cannot be read as a percentage or converted into one. What they do establish is spatial: mineralization is now widespread along the Hurricane South trend rather than confined to isolated holes. Samples are at SRC with no stated turnaround. What's missing: any assay from the summer program, any cps-to-grade correlation for these holes, and a date for either.

Terra North ResourcesTSXV:GEMC8 Sep

Prominent Radiometric Anomalies Identified at Charlot-Neely Lake

First look at a new airborne dataset over Terra North's Charlot-Neely Lake ground, about 20 km north of Uranium City. 2,441 line-km of high-resolution radiometric and magnetic survey across 163.7 km² resolved seven uranium-thorium radiometric anomalies, one of them north of Neely Lake where historical outcrop sampling reportedly ran up to 0.8% U₃O₈. No anomaly-by-anomaly dimensions or coordinates are given, and radiometrics read surface and near-surface signal only, not depth. The release comes from Global Energy Metals (TSXV:GEMC), which holds a 0.5% NSR royalty across Terra North's Saskatchewan portfolio rather than operating the ground - Terra North itself is private, holds an option on the project from Fulcrum Metals Canada Ltd., and is 39.5%-owned by Terra Balcanica Resources (CSE:TERA).

Belmont ResourcesTSXV:BEA10 Sep

Belmont Resources to Conduct Ground Gravity Survey at Crackingstone Uranium Project, Saskatchewan

A ground gravity program aimed at an under-tested corner of the historic Beaverlodge district: approximately 2,500 stations on a 50 m × 50 m grid across 253 ha at Crackingstone, about 8 km southwest of Uranium City, scheduled for the first week of October and expected to run 10-14 field days. The company holds a permit for up to 40 drill holes through 2028 and frames the survey as the lead-in to that program. Crackingstone's own historical record, cited in the release, includes showings up to 15.6% U₃O₈, with 11 tons mined at 2.3% U₃O₈ from the No. 7 Adit during the district's original production era - historical figures from past-producing workings, not from any drilling of Belmont's own. This is a mobilization notice for a survey that has not yet run; no gravity data exists yet.

Announces $2.0 Million Brokered Private Placement Led by Centurion One Capital

A modest raise with no catalyst attached. Up to 14,285,714 units at $0.14 (Centurion One Capital as lead agent, 8% cash fee plus broker warrants), with the agent holding an option for a further $300,000. Each unit carries a warrant exercisable at $0.22 for three years, with an acceleration clause if the stock trades above $0.44 for 15 straight days. Mobilizes Drill to Marshall Project, Athabasca Basin, Saskatchewan

First drilling under Green Canada's own name, on its first day trading following an RTO. Two holes are planned at the 11,225-hectare Marshall property, roughly 30 km southwest of CanAlaska's West McArthur Pike Zone, testing an unconformity-style target beneath sandstone cover. The company holds Marshall through a 51% option into North Millennium's underlying interest. No metreage, timeline or target dimensions are given - this is a mobilization notice, not a results release. Majors: an active week for CanAlaska, quiet elsewhere CanAlaska Uranium (TSXV:CVV) began its planned fall drill program at Key Extension on 8 September, following the single-rig mobilization reported the same day; six to ten holes are planned to late October, testing up-ice of the Orchid Lake boulder field along a western conductive trend with more than 10 km of sparsely tested conductive stratigraphy. Winter holes KEY017 and KEY018 hit graphitic stratigraphy with semi-brittle faulting and localized chlorite-clay alteration; those geochemical results are still outstanding. Historical Orchid Lake boulder grabs ran up to 0.13% uranium, and the release itself flags them as selective and not representative.

Denison Mines holds a 30% joint-venture interest in Cosa Resources' Murphy Lake North project (see above) but has not issued its own release on the results. Cameco, Orano, NexGen and Uranium Energy Corp issued no releases in the window. UEC has been silent since 23 July; the Roughrider pre-feasibility study is still unreleased, with the last substantive status the June quarterly that had drilling roughly 80% complete and Tetra Tech engaged. UEC's fiscal year ends 31 July, so full-year results around October are the next scheduled opening for it.
Flagged: trading halt continues, five sessions now Nexus Uranium Corp. (CSE:NEXU) remains halted by CIRO, all issues, since 10 September. The bulletin cites improper dissemination of news as the reason. No corrective release or resumption notice has been issued yet. Watching for either before treating this as more than a flag.
Out of basin, but on the watchlist ATHA Energy (TSXV:SASK), 8 September, Rib North at Angilak, Nunavut. Seven holes (RIBN-DD-010 to -016A) extend the mineralized corridor from the 300 m defined at discovery in 2025 to 1.45 km of strike on the eastern limb, open in all directions. RIBN-DD-011 returned 20.0 m of composite mineralization over fifteen zones between 494.0 m and 700.5 m, including a 1.5 m interval at 661.5-663.0 m averaging 8,453 cps with a 26,680 cps peak; RIBN-DD-013A returned 13.0 m composite over five zones, RIBN-DD-014 7.0 m over six. Every number is a downhole total-count reading from a Mount Sopris 40TGU-1000 probe, not a calibrated eU₃O₈ conversion and not a chemical assay, so none of it converts to a percentage - the company's own thresholds are 300 cps for mineralization and 10,000 cps for high grade. Angilak is Archean-hosted vein-style mineralization in the Kivalliq, geologically unrelated to the basin. Carried because ATHA holds Gemini and a large basin package; not ranked against basin results.

Manhattan Uranium Discovery (TSXV:MANU), 10 September, Lisbon Valley, Utah. A compilation of 499 historical drill holes returned a best of 3.70% eU₃O₃ over 5.2 ft, gamma-derived from legacy data, not new drilling. Sandstone-hosted Colorado Plateau style, unrelated to the basin. Carried because Manhattan operates Murmac and Strike on this roster.

Geiger Energy (TSXV:BEEP), 14 September, Apollo target at Aberdeen, Thelon Basin, Nunavut - not the company's Saskatchewan Aberdeen ground. Hole APL26-001 reached 384 m total depth, with elevated handheld scintillometer readings across two intervals (206-210 m, 216-228 m); the release itself states these counts are qualitative and not a substitute for a chemical assay. Carried here only to flag the naming overlap with Geiger's Hook/ACKIO/Aberdeen properties in the basin, which this release does not concern.
